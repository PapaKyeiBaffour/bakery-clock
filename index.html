All pay months

Jobs

Each job has its own hourly rate and its own pay month. A pay month is named after the month it ends in. For example, if a job's pay month usually ends on the 27th, October runs from 28 Sep to 27 Oct. When one month ends on a different day, change just that month in the Timesheet tab.

Export timesheet

Paste it into Excel or Google Sheets. Pay month and grand totals are included.

Add many shifts

Paste one shift per line, such as your old timesheet rows. Each line needs a date, an in time and an out time, for example Friday, July 31, 2026 3:00 AM 6:00 PM or 31/07/2026 03:00 18:00. Dates with a slash are read day first. All lines go to the job you pick here.

Backup and restore

Your hours are saved in this browser on this phone. Copy a backup now and then, and paste it back here to restore it on another phone or after clearing site data. Older backups still work.
